# capture-clip revision A: blind design review (2026-09-30)

Reviewer: a separate agent that did not build the design and did not read `DECISIONS.md` or
`research/2026-09-30-power-path-fix.md` (only two datasheet URLs were taken from the latter by grep).

How this was checked:
- Every pad's net was read from `boards/capture-clip/kicad/capture-clip.kicad_pcb` (parsed with a script; pad positions turned into board coordinates) and compared with `src/circuit.rs` and the datasheets.
- Tracks, vias and the filled pours were read from the same file; path lengths are the shortest routed path between two pads.
- Coordinates are layout mm from the board's top-left corner, Y down (as in `src/layout.rs`).
- Datasheets read for this review: HE9073 (HEERMICR Ver 1.3, LCSC C723789 mirror), RB168MM-40 (ROHM Rev.002, C509936 mirror), adafruit.com/product/3898. Everything else rests on `research/2026-09-30-datasheet-check-capture-clip.md`.
- Reports read: `erc.json` (0 items), `drc.json` (16 warnings, all waived silk items; 0 unconnected; parity empty), `routing.json`, `gates.json`, `case/fit.json` (all checks pass), `fab/*`.

Cost-of-a-miss classes: **firmware** (fixable in code), **rework** (fixable by hand on the delivered board, or by reprinting the case or buying a different cell), **new board**.

## Checked and found correct
- Pad → net for every IC and polarised part on the board: U2 (1 CHRG, 2 GND, 3 BAT, 4 VBUS, 5 STDBY, 6 PROG), Q1 (G VBUS, S VSYS, D +BATT; body diode cell → VSYS), D2 (pad 1 cathode on VSYS), D3 (pad 1 cathode on VBUS), D1, D4 (pad 4 anode on VSYS), U3, U4, MK1, J1 (D+ on A6/B6 → pad 14, D− on A7/B7 → pad 13, CC1/CC2 each on its own 5.1k), J2 (pad 1 +BATT, the "+" silk at x 25.9 is at pad 1), U1 pads against the pin map in `board.toml`.
- Power path logic, reversed-cell tolerance, no back-feed into USB, running from USB with no cell.
- Strapping: IO0 has 10k up and the button, no capacitor; IO3, IO45, IO46 unconnected. Flashing: native USB, or hold SW1 and poke SW2.
- Gerbers, drill file and CPL share one origin (board's bottom-left); vias are tented; smallest hole 0.3 mm; one-sided assembly.
- Cost: `cost.json` $66.89 for the design, $96.48 alone in a parcel, under the spec's $150 guide; 8 Extended parts ($24.56).

## What I could not verify
- The battery: lead exit position, real lead length, polarity of a pack in hand, its internal resistance.
- Whether the USB connector's legs protrude below the board (no drawing dimension read).
- HE9073 load-transient behaviour and its fold-back under a Wi-Fi burst; dropout maximum at 355 mA (only 200 mA has a maximum).
- The module's real deep-sleep current with 8 MB PSRAM; capacitor leakage.
- JLCPCB's own DFM result and placement preview (needs an upload); current stock beyond `cost.json`.
- The TP4057's package dimensions (datasheet page mislabelled, per the earlier check) and RB168MM-40's land pattern against `D_SOD-123F` (both SOD-123FL by name; dimensions not compared).
- Acoustic response, radio range, thermal behaviour: bench only.
- The case beyond `fit.json` and the three renders; the STEP/STL solids were not opened.

## Verdict
**Not ready to order.** No wrong pinout, polarity or footprint was found, and the gates that exist do pass. But the specified battery cannot be plugged in as laid out (finding 1), and the ground pour, regulator dropout, power track widths and surge-diode routing (findings 2–5) are all cheap to fix in a layout round now and awkward or impossible on a delivered board. Fix 1–6, re-run the gates, then re-review the changed areas.
