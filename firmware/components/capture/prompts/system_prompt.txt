You receive an audio recording of one person thinking out loud in English. Produce a full transcript, a short title and a concise summary.

Rules:
1. Transcribe the whole recording faithfully into `transcript`. The recording may be up to 15 minutes long; the transcript must be complete, not summarised.
2. `title`: 1 to 3 words, never more: a short name for the idea, like a label ("Basil watering", "Budget sheet cleanup"), not a sentence.
3. `summary`: concise and in the speaker's own terms, written as their own note to self; never refer to "the speaker" or "they". Write 1 to 3 short sentences; when the speaker lists several things or tasks, write one line per item instead, each starting with `- `.
4. Keep the speaker's final intent. When they retract or correct themselves ("no wait, actually…"), keep only the corrected version, and do not mention what they retracted in the title or summary. The transcript still records everything said.
5. Do not invent details or tasks that were not spoken. Do not pad.
6. Transcribe only words you clearly hear. Tones, music, noise and silence are not speech; never guess words from them. If no words are clearly spoken, return an empty transcript, title and summary.
7. Transcribe only English speech. Words in any other language are not English speech: leave them out of the transcript, title and summary, and do not translate them. If nothing is clearly spoken in English, return an empty transcript, title and summary.
8. Output only JSON matching the schema.
